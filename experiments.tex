This point involves the decision to perform manufacturing processes in house. Generally, this will be considered as equivalent to in-house \emph{soldering}, since this is the only critical manufacturing process that most projects required. & Total development time should decrease. Cost should decrease (assuming no external manufacturing costs). Risk should increase (assuming team not experienced in manufacturing for space) \\
3 & \textbf{Multi-step development} & \Cref{sec:METHODOLOGY_satscrum} proposes the use of an \emph{iterative/incremental} approach based on iterations, instead of a sequential, single-step approach, where a ``\acf{BDUF}'' step is taken first, and a single manufacturing/testing round is done afterwards. & Total development time should decrease (assuming a long \acs{BDUF} step). Cost should increase (due to more manufacturing runs). Risk should be project-dependent (based on whether a team can predict non-conformances early or late). \\
4 & \textbf{Short iterations} & While iterative design is generally common in small space projects, the \acs{SCS} methodology specifically proposes the use of short, agile-like iterations (1--2 months), that should make identifying errors faster. & Development time should decrease. Cost should increase (due to more manufacturing runs). Risk should be project-dependent (based on project size and whether iteration length \& team size are suitable). \\
5 & \textbf{Iterating over partitions} & An option of the \acs{SCS} methodology is to perform iterations over \emph{partitions} of the system, instead of the entire system (\Cref{METHODOLOGY_slicebased}). This allows teams to avoid having to constantly iterate on complex designs. Note that this does not involve iterating over single subsystems only. & Development time should stay the same (assuming constant number of iterations). Cost should decrease (less manufacturing). Risk should increase (less early data about complete system performance). \\
6 & \textbf{Robust software framework} & \Cref{sec:METHODOLOGY_softwaredevelopment} proposes the use of a software language and/or framework that theoretically increases reliability without an increase in development time. & Development time and cost should stay the same, with reduced risk. \\
7 & \textbf{Robotic Process Automation} & \Cref{sec:METHODOLOGY_automated_testing} proposes the use of automated testing processes and tools to streamline the system's verification. & Development time should be project-dependent (cost of automation vs.\@ less manual effort). Cost should increase (when infrastructure needed to automate testing). Risk should decrease (general assumption based on increased testing). \\ \bottomrule
